## Companion analysis

The companion analysis recalculates December 2017 profitability and liquidity ratios, including a **58.50% gross margin**, **21.00% operating margin**, and **1.04× current ratio**. In the displayed source units, closing assets of **14,666** equal liabilities of **11,926** plus equity of **2,740**. The [calculation review](assets/calculation-review.png) documents the adjustments and an open gross-profit-to-EBIT reconciliation question.

The original export and PBIX retain their displayed figures. The companion review uses company-labelled source data with currency, scale, and actual-versus-forecast classification still to be confirmed. The 2013–2014 sales sample is separate, and its forecast still needs validation.
